
# 📚 Clash of Research — AI for Research and Publication

## 📌 About This Repository

This repository documents my participation in the **Clash of Research — AI untuk Proposal, Analisis Data, dan Publikasi bagi Mahasiswa & Akademisi**, organized by MyData.

As an Information Technology student at **Politeknik Negeri Lampung**, I participated in this training to expand my knowledge of research methodology, data analysis, academic writing, and the ethical use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in research.

## 📖 Training Topics

The training covered the following competencies:

- **Research Proposal Writing:** Identifying research topics, research gaps, novelty, objectives, and methodology.
- **Data Analysis Methods:** Selecting suitable data types, analysis methods, and analytical tools.
- **Result Interpretation:** Developing academic discussions from analytical outputs with ethical AI assistance.
- **Academic Publication:** Preparing manuscripts for submission to SINTA or Scopus journals.
- **AI Prompting:** Applying prompting techniques to develop structured initial drafts of academic assignments from Chapter 1 to Chapter 5.

The training consisted of 16 synchronous hours and 16 asynchronous hours.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

Through this training, I aimed to strengthen my ability to:

- Develop structured and well-defined research proposals.
- Understand appropriate data analysis approaches.
- Use AI responsibly to support academic research.
- Interpret research results and communicate findings clearly.
- Understand the process of preparing academic manuscripts for publication.
